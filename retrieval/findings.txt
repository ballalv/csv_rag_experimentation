Overall Finding

The experiments show that Top-K and chunk size both affect retrieval quality, but increasing either one does not always improve the result.

For the Renal Cell Carcinoma query, chunk size 300 was the smallest tested configuration that retrieved the complete required information.

However, the Business Unit and Quantity/Sales Value queries remained incomplete even when the chunk size was increased to 1000.

Therefore, chunk size and Top-K alone are not sufficient to solve all retrieval problems.
